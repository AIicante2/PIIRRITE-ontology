Pedestrian mobility poses major challenges in modern society and requires a \emph{design for all} approach~\cite{cencenelec2024} capable of meeting everyone’s diverse needs. Whether navigating a university campus, visiting a museum, or exploring a downtown area, pedestrians can greatly benefit from route-planning solutions that go beyond simply optimizing the shortest route, by taking into account the specific needs, characteristics, and preferences of each user. Such an inclusive system requires a thorough description of the traversed environment and its contextual properties —wheelchair accessibility, protection from adverse weather, elevator availability, noise reduction or proximity to particular points of interest such as rest areas or amenities.

Area of Interestc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: http://piirrite.univ-lyon1.fr/ontology/core#AreaOfInterest

Comparison operatorc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: http://piirrite.univ-lyon1.fr/ontology/core#ComparisonOperator

An operator usable for the matching between a validity condition and the value of a contextual property. e.g. equality, membership, difference, superiority, etc.
is equivalent to
{ equals ni , greater than ni , less than ni }
has super-classes
Operator c
has members
equals ni, greater than ni, less than ni
